About The Pet Shop

We've been matching pets with families in this neighborhood since 2004. Every animal that comes through our doors is vet-checked, vaccinated, and cared for by our team until they find a home.

Our story

The Pet Shop started as a single storefront run by a husband-and-wife team who couldn't say no to a stray. Today we work with local rescues and small breeders to place dogs and cats with families across the area, and every animal is vet-checked before it's ever put up for adoption.

What we offer

Adoptions, basic veterinary checks, starter food and supplies, and free advice on choosing a pet that fits your home and schedule. If you're not sure what's right for you, stop by and one of our staff will walk you through it.
