Tomato & Basil Pasta

A comforting classic for an easy weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • A handful of fresh basil, salt, and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook pasta according to the package directions.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and gently cook garlic for 1 minute. Add tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Toss with drained pasta and basil. Season to taste.

10 minutes · Serves 2

Fresh Garden Salad

A crisp, colorful side with a simple lemon dressing.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups washed mixed greens
  • 1 diced cucumber
  • 1 cup halved cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice, salt, and pepper

Steps

  1. Place greens, cucumber, and tomatoes in a bowl.
  2. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper together.
  3. Toss with the dressing just before serving.

20 minutes · Serves 2

Fluffy Breakfast Pancakes

A warm and cheerful start to your morning.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • ¾ cup milk and 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter, plus extra for the pan

Steps

  1. Mix flour, baking powder, and sugar. Whisk milk, egg, and melted butter separately.
  2. Combine wet and dry ingredients until just mixed.
  3. Pour small portions onto a buttered pan over medium heat. Flip when bubbles appear; cook until golden and the centers are cooked through.
